# Quantum Cyber Defense

A research prototype comparing quantum and classical reinforcement learning
for defensive decision-making in a synthetic six-asset environment.

The project compares a hybrid quantum policy trained with REINFORCE against
a rule-based controller, DQN, and PPO.

In the saved exploratory experiment, DQN performed best.
The quantum policy ran on a CPU simulator; no quantum advantage was demonstrated.
